Debunking Common Misinformation

  • "The Bible explicitly states that being LGBTQ+ is an abomination."
    • There are a total of 6 verses in the Bible that discuss anything regarding LGBTQ+ people/being LGBTQ+, and each of them are mistranslated and/or misinterpreted. In fact, the word "homosexuality", which is stated in many of these translated verses, was actually never present in the original texts.
  • "Sexual orientation is a choice, and can be changed."
    • There have been several studies that have found out that sexual orientation is not a choice, but could potentially be linked to biology and genetics, although a general answer regarding this has not been reached yet(Look at the American Psychological Association and similar sites for more information). Likewise, sexual orientations cannot be changed, since they were never choices to begin with, which is why practices like Gay Conversion Therapy are not supported by (See the Human Rights Campaign)
